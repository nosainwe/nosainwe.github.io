Privacy Policy

Tech with the Geek WhatsApp and phone assistant · Last updated 24 November 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you message or call Tech with the Geek (Nosa Peter Inwe) on the business WhatsApp number +358 44 955 3069 or on a business phone line. An automated assistant answers first; I read the conversations and step in myself when needed.

What I collect

  • Your WhatsApp number or caller number, and the profile name WhatsApp shares with businesses.
  • The messages you send and the assistant's replies. For phone calls, a text transcript and a short summary. Calls are not audio-recorded.
  • Anything you give to book a meeting or request a job: your name, email if you share it, the topic, preferred times, budget and deadline.

I don't ask for payment details, ID documents or passwords over WhatsApp or by phone, and the assistant never takes payments.

Why I use it

Only to answer your questions about my services, book and remind you about meetings you asked for, log the work you request, and follow up on it. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me (Art. 6(1)(f)). I don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or add you to marketing lists.

Who processes it for me

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries the messages between you and the assistant.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI models) reads the conversation to write the assistant's replies. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data isn't used to train its models.
  • Cloudflare hosts the assistant and its database (messages, bookings, orders).
  • Google Calendar holds the meetings you book: your name, the topic and the time.
  • For phone calls only: Twilio or Telnyx (the phone line) and Deepgram (speech-to-text and text-to-speech).

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep it

Conversation history, bookings and orders are kept while we're in contact and for as long as I need them to handle your request or a follow-up. I review and delete old records periodically, and you can ask me to delete yours at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or its deletion, and you can object to how it's used. Message the WhatsApp number with "delete my data", or email nosa.inwe@uef.fi. I'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your national data protection authority. In Finland that's the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi), and in France the CNIL (cnil.fr).
